Visual direction 03

Focused Instrument

The reader behaves like a calibrated research instrument: cool luminance steps, fine borders, tabular metadata, and compact controls keep state legible at a glance. Light and dark are tuned as parallel working environments, with the sentence link acting as the precise point of measurement.

Best fit

Frequent users who prioritize compact information density, exact state recognition, and a tool-forward sense of control.

Tradeoff

The technical register can feel less contemplative during long reading sessions and may need careful warmth in later content surfaces.

3 · Model Architecture

3.2.1 Scaled Dot-Product Attention

We call our particular attention “Scaled Dot-Product Attention.” The input consists of queries and keys of dimension dk, and values of dimension dv.

We compute the dot products of the query with all keys, divide each by √dk, and apply a softmax function to obtain the weights on the values.

Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax(QKᵀ / √dk)V

We suspect that for large values of dk, the dot products grow large in magnitude, pushing the softmax function into regions where it has extremely small gradients. To counteract this effect, we scale the dot products by 1 / √dk.

In practice, we compute the attention function on a set of queries simultaneously, packed together into a matrix Q.

3.2.1 · 문장 정렬됨

스케일드 닷-프로덕트 어텐션

우리는 이 어텐션을 “스케일드 닷-프로덕트 어텐션”이라고 부른다. 입력은 차원이 dk인 쿼리와 키, 그리고 차원이 dv인 값으로 구성된다.

쿼리와 모든 키의 내적을 계산하고, 각각을 √dk로 나눈 뒤, softmax 함수를 적용해 값에 대한 가중치를 얻는다.

우리는 dk 값이 클수록 내적의 크기가 커져 softmax 함수가 기울기가 극도로 작은 영역으로 밀려난다고 본다. 이 효과를 상쇄하기 위해 내적을 1 / √dk로 스케일링한다.

실제 구현에서는 여러 쿼리를 행렬 Q에 함께 묶어 어텐션 함수를 동시에 계산한다.
